IT Helpdesk & Asset Management System

Web-based IT service management system for structured incident tracking, IT asset inventory, maintenance scheduling, and SLA-aware performance monitoring — built to replace informal IT support workflows with a traceable, data-driven process.

01

Problem Statement

Understanding the root cause before designing any solution.

⚠️

Pain Points Identified

  • IT incident reports received via WhatsApp, email, and verbal requests — no single intake channel
  • No ticket tracking — issues are forgotten, duplicated, or never resolved
  • Technician workload is invisible — no way to see who is handling what
  • IT asset inventory stored in spreadsheets that are rarely updated
  • No record of which device is assigned to whom or its current condition
  • Maintenance schedules exist only in the technician's head — preventive maintenance is skipped
  • Management has zero visibility into IT service performance
🎯

Goals & Expected Outcomes

  • Centralized IT ticket intake with structured categorization and priority
  • Full ticket lifecycle tracking: Open → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Closed
  • Clear technician assignment and workload visibility
  • Comprehensive IT asset registry with condition, location, and assigned-user tracking
  • Maintenance scheduling with history per asset
  • SLA enforcement — auto-flag overdue tickets and alert IT leads
  • Dashboard with KPIs so management can monitor IT operations performance

"The biggest problem in IT support isn't technical complexity — it's the absence of structure. When nothing is tracked, nothing improves."

— Observation from IT Support Internship at Pengadilan Agama Tanjung Pati, Jan–Mar 2025

Business Process

Mapping how incidents flow from report to resolution, and how assets are managed across their lifecycle.

Incident Ticket Flow

01
📩

Report

Employee submits incident via web form with category, priority, and description

02
🔍

Triage

IT Admin reviews ticket, validates priority, assigns SLA target and technician

03
⚙️

Handle

Technician diagnoses, troubleshoots, and logs action notes in the ticket

04
✅

Resolve

Ticket marked Resolved with documented solution; SLA compliance calculated

05
📋

Close

Reporter confirms resolution; ticket archived with full audit trail

Asset Lifecycle Flow

01
📦

Register

New asset entered with full spec, serial number, and purchase/warranty data

02
🤝

Assign

Asset assigned to department or user; status changes to Assigned

03
🔧

Maintain

Scheduled or corrective maintenance logged with technician notes and outcome

04
🔄

Transfer

Asset reassigned or moved; transfer history recorded with date and reason

05
🚫

Retire

End-of-life asset flagged as Retired with disposal notes and date

👤

Employee (Reporter)

  • Submit IT incident or service request
  • Track own ticket status
  • Confirm resolution and close ticket
  • View assigned assets
💻

IT Technician

  • View and handle assigned tickets
  • Update ticket status and add action notes
  • Perform and log asset maintenance
  • Mark tickets as Resolved
🧑‍💼

IT Admin

  • Full ticket management and assignment
  • Manage asset registry and categories
  • Monitor SLA compliance dashboard
  • Generate reports and manage users/roles

4 Known limitations identified
Frontend-only prototype Current version is a static HTML/CSS/JS simulation. Full backend (Laravel + MySQL) is planned but not yet implemented.
No real-time notifications Email/push notifications for SLA breach and technician assignment are designed but not yet integrated in the prototype.
No file attachment handling The UI supports attachment upload fields, but file storage backend logic is pending implementation.
Simulated data only All ticket and asset data shown in the prototype is pre-defined. Live database integration is planned for v2.
